Nothing is uploaded

The comparison engine is the same Rust core that ships in the desktop app, compiled to WebAssembly and run inside this tab. Your documents are read by JavaScript in your own browser and never sent to a server — the privacy policy says so in writing.

/ QUESTIONS

Are my documents uploaded to a server?

No. The comparison engine is WebAssembly that runs inside the page, on your own device. Your documents are read by JavaScript in your browser and are never sent to us or to any third party.

What do I get back?

A .docx file with genuine Word tracked revisions — insertions, deletions, moves and formatting changes — that Microsoft Word can accept, reject, and filter by author.

# Master Services Agreement

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into by
Cedar House Studio LLC ("Contractor") and Orbit Logistics Ltd. ("Client").

## 1. Services

Contractor will furnish support as described in each Statement of Work:

- Design system maintenance and component library work
- Engineering support for Client's storefront releases

Each Statement of Work forms part of this Agreement.

## 2. Fees

Client shall pay Contractor the fees stated in each Statement of Work,
net thirty days from the invoice date. Late payments accrue interest
at 1.5 percent per month.

## 3. Confidentiality

Each party shall protect the other's Confidential Information with no less
care than it uses for its own. The receiving party shall return or destroy
Confidential Information on the disclosing party's written request.

## 4. Term and Termination

This Agreement begins on February 1, 2026 and runs until either party ends
it with ninety days written notice.

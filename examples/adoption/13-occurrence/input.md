# Records Handling Addendum

This Addendum governs the handling of Records under the Master Services
Agreement between the parties.

## Handling Rules

The Advisor shall store the Records in a secure facility. **The Advisor
shall tag** each Record with its classification level before storage.
The Advisor shall log every access in the access register.

## Register Entries

Each register entry records:

- the Record's identifier and classification level
- who accessed it, and when
- the custody chain for transferred Records

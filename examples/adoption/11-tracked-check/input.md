# Consulting Agreement (short form)

This Agreement is made between Jandira Technologies, LLC (the "Company")
and Northwind Traders Ltd. (the "Client") on 2 March 2026.

## Services

The Company will advise the Client on document automation: pipeline design,
template review, and acceptance testing of the generated documents.

## Fees

The Company will invoice the Client monthly. The fee is nine hundred USD
per day, payable net thirty (30) days. The pilot phase is billed at half
rate.

## Term

Either party may end this Agreement on twenty days' written notice.

# Consulting Services Letter

This letter confirms the consulting engagement between Jandira
Technologies, LLC ("the Company") and Northwind Traders Ltd. (the
"Client"), starting on 2 March 2026.

## Services

The Company will provide document-automation consulting up to ten days per
month, remotely, with one on-site day per quarter.

## Fees

The fee is 900 USD per day, invoiced monthly, payable net thirty (30) days.

## Term

Either party may end this letter on twenty days' written notice to the
other address listed below.

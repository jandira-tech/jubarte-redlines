# Consulting Services Letter

This letter confirms the consulting engagement between Jandira
Technologies, LLC ("the Company") and Northwind Traders Ltd. (the
"Client"), starting on 16 March 2026.

## Services

The Company will provide document-automation consulting up to fifteen days
per month, remotely, with one on-site day per quarter.

## Fees

The fee is 1,100 USD per day, invoiced monthly, payable net thirty (30)
days.

## Term

Either party may end this letter on twenty days' written notice to the
address in the signature block.

# Consulting Services Letter

<!-- p1 -->
This letter confirms the consulting engagement between Jandira Technologies, LLC ("the Company") and Northwind Traders Ltd. (the "Client"), starting on 16 March 2026.

<!-- p2 -->
## Services

<!-- p3 -->
The Company will provide document-automation consulting up to fifteen days per month, remotely, with one on-site day per quarter.

<!-- p4 -->
## Fees

<!-- p5 -->
The fee is 1,100 USD per day, invoiced monthly, payable net thirty (30) days.

<!-- p6 -->
## Term

<!-- p7 -->
Either party may end this letter on twenty days' written notice to the address in the signature block.

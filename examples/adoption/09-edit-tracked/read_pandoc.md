# Consulting Services Agreement

This Consulting Services Agreement (the \"Agreement\") is entered into
by Northwind Analytics LLC (\"Consultant\") and Fabrikam Services Inc.
(\"Client\").

## 1. Scope of Work

Consultant will provide the following services during the term:

- Monthly performance reporting across the Client\'s sales channels

- Quarterly business reviews with the executive team

- Ad-hoc analysis requests, up to ten hours per month

## 2. Fees and Payment

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Item                    Amount                  Due
  ----------------------- ----------------------- -----------------------

  Ad-hoc hours beyond ten \$180/hour              With monthly invoice
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

Client shall pay all invoices within fifteen days of receipt.

## 3. Confidentiality

Consultant shall keep the Client\'s information confidential and use it
only for the Permitted Purpose. Consultant shall return or destroy all
confidential information within ten days after termination.

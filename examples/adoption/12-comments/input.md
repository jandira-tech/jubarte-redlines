# Non-Disclosure Agreement

This Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into by
Meridian Labs Inc. ("Discloser") and Harbor Tools GmbH ("Recipient").

## 1. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any information disclosed by Discloser
to Recipient, in any form, that is marked confidential or that
reasonably should be understood to be confidential given the nature of
the information and the disclosure.

## 2. Obligations

Recipient shall hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence
and shall:

- use it only for the Permitted Purpose;
- protect it with no less care than its own similar information;
- notify Discloser promptly of any unauthorized disclosure.

Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to its employees who
need it for the Permitted Purpose, provided each employee signs a
confidentiality agreement at least as protective as this Agreement.

## 3. Term

This Agreement remains in force for three years from the Effective Date.

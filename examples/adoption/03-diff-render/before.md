# Editorial Engagement Letter

Between Jandira Technologies, LLC ("Jandira") and the Cortado Review
("the Publisher"), dated 10 February 2026.

## 1. Engagement

Jandira will copyedit the Publisher's quarterly review, issues 41
through 44, on the schedule attached to this letter. Each issue is
delivered as a single document of roughly eighty manuscript pages,
with figures, tables and pull quotes already placed by the Publisher's
designer.

## 2. What the edit covers

1. Spelling, punctuation and grammar against the attached house sheet
2. Consistency of cross-references, figure numbers and captions
3. Table alignment and column heads, flagged, not reset
4. A marked-up copy with every change visible, plus a clean copy

The edit does not cover rewriting for style, fact-checking, or
permissions clearance for quoted material. Where a sentence is
grammatical but ambiguous, Jandira flags it in a margin note instead
of choosing a reading on the Publisher's behalf.

## 3. Schedule

| Issue | Manuscript due | Marked copy due |
|---|---|---|
| 41 | 3 March | 17 March |
| 42 | 2 June | 16 June |
| 43 | 8 September | 22 September |
| 44 | 1 December | 15 December |

Dates shift only by written agreement. A manuscript that arrives late
moves its marked copy by the same number of working days.

## 4. Fee

The fee is 2,400.00 USD per issue, invoiced on delivery of the marked
copy and due net 30. An issue that runs more than ten percent over the
agreed length is quoted separately before work begins, not after.

## 5. Review and acceptance

The Publisher reviews each marked copy within ten working days and
returns either an acceptance or a list of objections. Jandira answers
objections once per issue without further charge. Unanswered
objections after a further ten working days are treated as accepted.

## 6. Rights and confidentiality

Jandira keeps the manuscripts in confidence and gains no rights in
them; upon payment, all editorial suggestions belong to the Publisher.
Either party may end this letter for convenience on fourteen days'
written notice, with work completed to that date invoiced at the
per-issue rate. This letter is governed by the laws of Portugal.

# Master Services Agreement

Between Jandira Technologies, LLC ("Jandira") and Halyard Press, Inc.
("the Client"), dated 3 March 2026.

## 1. Scope of services

Jandira will deliver the document redline engine described in statement
of work 2026-04, in three phases:

1. Discovery and fixture collection
2. Layout parity against Microsoft Word
3. Handover of the benchmark harness

Each phase ends with a written milestone report and a demonstration over
the shared fixture corpus. Work in one phase begins only after the Client
accepts the previous milestone in writing.

## 2. Fees and schedule

| Phase | Deliverable | Weeks | Fee (USD) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fixture corpus and parity report | 4 | 12,000 |
| 2 | Layout engine parity pass | 8 | 18,000 |
| 3 | Benchmark harness handover | 3 | 9,000 |
| | Total | 15 | 39,000 |

Invoices are issued on milestone acceptance and are due net 30. Late
payment bears interest at 1.0% per month, to the extent permitted by law.

## 3. Confidentiality

Each party will keep the other's confidential information in confidence
and use it only to perform this agreement. This obligation survives
termination by two years.

## 4. Term and termination

This agreement runs for twelve months from the date above and renews for
successive three-month terms unless either party gives thirty days'
written notice. Either party may terminate for material breach not cured
within fifteen days of written notice.

## 5. Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without
regard to its conflict-of-law provisions. Any dispute arising under it
shall be settled by binding arbitration in New York County before a
single arbitrator under the CPR Rules.

## 2. Independently checked arithmetic from the reports

These calculations use **the supplied printed tables**, parsed and checked with Python fractions/statistics. They are not a reconstruction of the underlying 114 MB run corpus. F1 means `upload/final_turn_001.md`; F2 means `upload/final_turn_002.md`.

| Report claim | Exact reference | Check and permissible conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Median first edit is 89% | F2:118–130 | The nine printed percentages are 24, 46, 50, 57, 64, 89, 93, 93, 99. Their median is **64%**. Using the actual turn fractions gives **63.6363636%** (70/110). The published 89% does not follow from this table. |
| All nine rows are gate-failed | F2:116–128 versus 217–221 | `iNcXKY8` is listed at 62/108 but subsequently described as gate-passed with 31 operations and zero rubric credit. Excluding it gives eight rows: median **76.5%** from printed percentages, or **76.461039%** from exact fractions. This is a conditional table correction, not a certified corpus estimate. |
| Eight versus fourteen never invoked edit/add | F1:23,35; F2:96,120–128,350 | Six of F2's fourteen alleged nonediting runs appear in its edit table: `US2zawW`, `7R7A5GW`, `XmK2wgS`, `KAGcp6f`, `RjnX6Cy`, `LqPodoU`. Eight nonediting plus eight editing is a coherent reconstruction after removing the gate-passed row, but requires the raw ledger to establish. Do not silently choose fourteen as a corrected truth. |
| Same mean .319 for 37 positive runs and 40 gate-passed runs including three zeros | F1:13–17; F2:86–90 | If the 37 positive mean is .319, the 40-run mean is **.295075**. If the 40-run mean is .319, the 37-run positive mean is **.344864865**. Ordinary three-decimal rounding cannot reconcile the two reported means. |
| 58 of 75 read SKILL, twelve never did | F1:65–67; F2:194 | 75 − 58 = **17**, not twelve. Twelve may be judged nonreaders and five unjudged nonreaders, but no cross-tab establishes that. A reader/nonreader score comparison must identify its scored denominators; exposure is not randomized. |
| Sixteen gate-failed documents were untouched/empty | F1:3,9–17; F2:78–90,350–352 | 16/56 = **28.5714% of judged runs**. The quoted predicate is absence of expected-author revisions/comments, not byte equality, document emptiness, or absence of all editing. The report also says the judged artifact path was not recorded. |
| “Each run” has 2,531 calls | F1:7,140; F2:74,337 | Later text uses 2,531 as the corpus-wide tool-result denominator. Correct the unit or leave it unresolved; do not multiply a corpus aggregate by 75. |
| 306/454 rubric items failed | F1:19; F2:92 | This arithmetic is coherent: **67.400881%**; the five category numerators and denominators sum correctly. It is pooled item failure, not a mean run score, and correlated rubric items are not independent tasks. |

F2:130 also conflates first edit and first dry run: `7R7A5GW` first edit is turn 70/110, whereas its first dry run is reported as 98/110. F1:125 and F2:289's separate 78%-of-budget median has no supporting event table and cannot be recovered from the first-edit percentages.
